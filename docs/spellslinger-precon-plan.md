# Plan: every precon playable, grouped, with its own place in Campaign

Owner's request (2026-10-08): "build them all and have them group appropriately so we can play those decks when we want, and also have their own spot in Campaign."

## What "all precons" means
The 553 decks of the nine kinds the game already uses (`PRECON_TYPES`, from MTGJSON): Welcome Decks 55, Starter Decks (1990s) 20, Theme Decks 220, Intro Packs 167, Starter Kits 12, Arena Starter Kits 8, Planeswalker Decks 41, Challenger Decks 22, Pioneer Challenger Decks 8. Together they use 7,647 distinct cards (basic lands left out). Not included: Commander decks, Jumpstart, Duel Decks, Secret Lair and the other deck products MTGJSON lists. Add a kind by adding it to `PRECON_TYPES`, `PRECON_GROUPS` and `CIRCUIT_LANES`.

## Where it stands (2026-10-08)
- (start of the work) 91 of 553 precons are made only of fully Automated cards ("Ready"): all Welcome Decks, Starter Kits and Arena Starter Kits, plus a few Theme Decks, Intro Packs and 1990s Starter Decks. 71 more are within 3 cards.
- 3,212 distinct cards still aren't fully Automated: 2,550 are castable but part of the text does nothing, 662 can't be cast. The tail is long (3,113 different unread lines), so most cards are needed by only one or two decks.

## Done
1. **Measure.** `python3 tests/precon_audit.py` downloads the decks once; `node tests/precon_audit.js` prints readiness and the cards that matter most; `--write` saves `spellslinger/data/precon-ready.json`, which the game reads to know which decks are Ready. **Rerun with `--write` after every rules round** and commit the file (like `CARD_COVERAGE`).
2. **📚 Precon Library** (Sandbox tab, `13b-precon-library.js`): all decks grouped by kind and then by set, a Ready badge or "N cards not fully working" (with the names when 3 or fewer), filters (kind, Ready only, search), pick any deck as the opponent and play it against one of your decks or a Ready precon. A deck that isn't Ready can still be the opponent (cards the game can't cast become basics, as before) but can't be your own.
3. **🃏 Precon Circuit** (in the Campaign tab): a lane per kind, a stop for every Ready deck, so it grows as decks are built. First win at a stop pays the lane's coins (20-100), repeat wins 10, a loss 5; progress is saved as `pc-<file>` in `campaignProgress`. AI level per lane: Welcome / 1990s Starter easy, Theme / Intro / Kits / Planeswalker normal, Challenger hard. These numbers are mine; change `CIRCUIT_LANES`.

## Round 1, part 1 (2026-10-08): 133 more cards Automated (12,380 -> 12,513), 124 of 553 precons Ready (was 91)
Pack `rules/precons1.js` and engine cases in `15b-precon-effects.js` (reached through a `default:` in `applyEffect`). New extension points in the core: `COND_EXTRA` (extra conditions), `STATIC_FN` (static-ability filters), `BLOCK_FN` (block restrictions), `TRIG_FN` entries, `preconDamage` (replacement and prevention: Furnace of Rath, Urza's Armor, Pariah, Vigor, Cho-Manno, Ghostly Possession, Vengeful Archon), activated abilities limited per turn (`maxTurn`), and `tests/suites/cards.js precon` (643 cards from the Ready decks played with no errors). **Approximations to know:** Fertile Ground / Verdant Haven add one extra mana of the land's own color; Erg Raiders treats "didn't attack" as "is untapped"; Mark for Death makes the other creatures unable to block (the AI isn't forced to block with the target); Relentless Assault starts its extra combat itself only when cast in the second main phase on your own turn.

## Round 1, part 2 (2026-10-08): 12,513 -> 12,621 Automated, 156 of 553 precons Ready
About 45 more cards, with new core hooks: `COND_EXTRA` conditions (it was kicked, you've been attacked this step, main phase before combat), `castCond` (cast restrictions), sorcery-as-flash, life-gain bonus (Angel of Vitality), attack and block taxes from Auras (Oppressive Rays, also +{3} to activated abilities), forced blocks (Lure), end-of-combat destroys (Deathgazer, via a wrapper around `combatDamage`), discard punishments (Guerrilla Tactics), land locks (Mole Worms), petrification (Xathrid Gorgon), new activated costs (tap two creatures, sacrifice a nontoken creature, tap a Spider) and per-turn activation limits. 1,080 cards of the Ready decks were played with no errors (`cards.js precon`) and `engine.js` has 31 behaviour checks for the round. **Approximations:** Oppressive Rays checks that its controller *can* pay {3} to block but does not charge it; Crypt Rats doesn't limit X to black mana; Mole Worms always keeps the land locked while it holds one; Gutter Grime's Oozes keep their size if it leaves; Tablet of the Guilds picks the two colors the player's cards use most.

**Not done from the first 128:** Blood Tribute (kicker by tapping a Vampire), and the planeswalker cards (Sarkhan, Dragonsoul; Sorin, Vampire Lord; Jiang Yanggu; Journey for the Elixir; Sarkhan's Whelp; Vivien's Jaguar; Sacred White Deer), which need their planeswalker abilities read. Next: those, then the repeating mechanics (Morph, Bushido, Soulshift, Buyback, Madness, Suspend...), then the tail (about 3,000 cards).

## Round 2 (2026-10-09): 12840 Automated, 173 of 553 precons Ready
Pack `rules/precons2.js` (counterspell filters for sorcery / creature-or-sorcery / spells that target a creature, Power Sink, Blightning, Exhaustion, Cruel Ultimatum, battle cry, unblocked-attacker triggers (new `unblocked` event), "before attackers are declared" abilities in `parseActivated`, planeswalker-type conditions ("you control a Teferi planeswalker"), Circle of Protection, Staff Magi, Guan Yu, Meglonoth, Hamletback Goliath, Spirit of the Hearth...).
