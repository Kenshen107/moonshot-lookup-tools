# Plan: every precon playable, grouped, with its own place in Campaign

Owner's request (2026-10-08): "build them all and have them group appropriately so we can play those decks when we want, and also have their own spot in Campaign."

## What "all precons" means
The 553 decks of the nine kinds the game already uses (`PRECON_TYPES`, from MTGJSON): Welcome Decks 55, Starter Decks (1990s) 20, Theme Decks 220, Intro Packs 167, Starter Kits 12, Arena Starter Kits 8, Planeswalker Decks 41, Challenger Decks 22, Pioneer Challenger Decks 8. Together they use 7,647 distinct cards (basic lands left out). Not included: Commander decks, Jumpstart, Duel Decks, Secret Lair and the other deck products MTGJSON lists. Add a kind by adding it to `PRECON_TYPES`, `PRECON_GROUPS` and `CIRCUIT_LANES`.

## Where it stands (2026-10-08)
- 91 of 553 precons are made only of fully Automated cards ("Ready"): all Welcome Decks, Starter Kits and Arena Starter Kits, plus a few Theme Decks, Intro Packs and 1990s Starter Decks. 71 more are within 3 cards.
- 3,212 distinct cards still aren't fully Automated: 2,550 are castable but part of the text does nothing, 662 can't be cast. The tail is long (3,113 different unread lines), so most cards are needed by only one or two decks.

## Done
1. **Measure.** `python3 tests/precon_audit.py` downloads the decks once; `node tests/precon_audit.js` prints readiness and the cards that matter most; `--write` saves `spellslinger/data/precon-ready.json`, which the game reads to know which decks are Ready. **Rerun with `--write` after every rules round** and commit the file (like `CARD_COVERAGE`).
2. **📚 Precon Library** (Sandbox tab, `13b-precon-library.js`): all decks grouped by kind and then by set, a Ready badge or "N cards not fully working" (with the names when 3 or fewer), filters (kind, Ready only, search), pick any deck as the opponent and play it against one of your decks or a Ready precon. A deck that isn't Ready can still be the opponent (cards the game can't cast become basics, as before) but can't be your own.
3. **🃏 Precon Circuit** (in the Campaign tab): a lane per kind, a stop for every Ready deck, so it grows as decks are built. First win at a stop pays the lane's coins (20-100), repeat wins 10, a loss 5; progress is saved as `pc-<file>` in `campaignProgress`. AI level per lane: Welcome / 1990s Starter easy, Theme / Intro / Kits / Planeswalker normal, Challenger hard. These numbers are mine; change `CIRCUIT_LANES`.
