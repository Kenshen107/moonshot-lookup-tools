# Spellslinger Duels: history of the rules work

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-08 (Phase 0 of `docs/spellslinger-overhaul-plan.md`) so that every session doesn't have to read it. The entries are unchanged and in their original order. Each entry says what a round of card-reader work added, the shared pieces it built (with the function names), the checks that were run and the coverage numbers at the time.

**The test scripts these entries mention (`mrd.js`, `m10.js`, `sand.js` and so on) were lost with the sessions that wrote them.** `tests/` now holds a rebuilt harness (see `tests/README.md`): rule checks, a card-by-card test, a coverage measurement and AI games. The numbers below are the numbers measured then.

CLAUDE.md keeps the short version: how the card reader is laid out, how to measure coverage, and the rule for adding a deck to `VERIFIED_DECKS`.

- **Rules round 2 (2026-10-05, owner's request: aim for 85-100% of cards):** built like Forge's card scripts and Arena's per-card rules: every ability = a trigger or a cost + effects from `EFFECTS`, so one new piece unlocks many cards. Code in the "Rules round 2" block (`parseTrigger`, `parseActivated`, `lordType`, `fire` / `flushTriggers` / `settle`, `activate`, `permMatches`, `applyChoiceEffect`).
  - **Triggers** (`TRIGGERS`): upkeep, end step, beginning of combat, attacks, blocks, dies, deals combat damage to a player, whenever you cast (filters), landfall, another creature enters, a creature dies, you gain life, you draw. Events queue triggers (`fire`); they go on the stack at the next safe point (`settle`, after each resolution in `runStack`), as in 603.3. A turn caps at 250 triggers.
  - **Activated abilities** (`parseActivated`): costs of mana, {T}, Sacrifice ~ / a creature / an artifact, Pay N life, Discard a card; "Activate only as a sorcery / once each turn". Your card popup shows ⚡ buttons (`abilityButtonsHTML`); the AI uses them (`aiUseAbilities`). Also **Crew N** (`crewPick`, `aiCrew`), **Cycling**, **Flashback** (cast from the graveyard, then exiled; tap 🪦 to see a graveyard) and **planeswalker loyalty** abilities (once a turn, sorcery speed; planeswalkers enter with loyalty and die at 0, but creatures can't attack them yet).
  - **Statics:** lords and anthems ("Other Elves you control get +1/+1", "Creatures you control have flying"; `staticBuffs`), can't block, can't be blocked, can't be countered, enters with +1/+1 counters, doesn't untap, attacks each combat if able, ward (the caster pays or the spell is countered), toxic.
  - **Keywords:** prowess, infect, wither, exalted, persist, undying, shadow, horsemanship, fear, intimidate, skulk, landwalk, changeling (devoid and partner have no effect here). Regenerate shields.
  - **Effects:** destroy / exile / bounce / untap any permanent type (with color and power filters), board wipes, team pumps and counters, edicts, loot and rummage, each player draws / loses, fetch basic lands (Cultivate, Rampant Growth), tutors, regrowth and reanimation (you pick from a list; the AI takes its best), Treasure / Food / Clue / Blood / Map / Powerstone tokens, fight, steal until end of turn, fog, proliferate, self-mill, exile from graveyards, Brainstorm, Swords to Plowshares life, modal "Choose one —" spells and enters abilities (`pickMode`), X spells (you pick X; the AI uses the most it can pay).

- **Rules round 3 (2026-10-06):** a real **mana pool** (rituals like Dark Ritual add mana that empties at the end of the step; `P.pool`, `emptyPools`), **painlands** and Ancient Tomb-style lands (life only for the colors that hurt; `mana.pain`), **convoke**, **kicker** (you're asked whether to pay it; the AI kicks when it can; "if it was kicked" effects and "deals N damage instead"; `totalCost`, `canKick`), **additional costs** (sacrifice a creature/artifact, discard, pay life; `addCostOk`, `payAddCost`), **X creatures** ("enters with X +1/+1 counters"), **counting P/T** (*/* "equal to the number of..." and "+1/+1 for each ..."; `countFn`, `basePT`), **Sagas** (lore counters each precombat main, chapter abilities, sacrificed after the last; `sagaChapter`, rule 714), **the monarch** (draw at your end step; combat damage to the monarch takes it), real **scry / surveil** (you choose top or bottom/graveyard for each card; `pickScry`), **dig** ("look at the top N, put one in your hand, the rest on the bottom", `§dig`), **copy target instant or sorcery spell**, Knights/Elves/... "you control get +N/+N until end of turn", old-style lords that boost every creature of a type ("Other Merfolk get +1/+1"; `global` statics), "can't be blocked this turn".
  - **Coverage** (measured on 2026-10-06, same method): fully automated 18.7% of all paper cards and 28.5% of the top 1,000; castable 83.4% of the top 1,000.

- **Rules round 4 (2026-10-06):** **creature lands** ("~ becomes a 2/2 creature until end of turn", `o.animated`; Mutavault, Inkmoth Nexus), **impulse draw** ("exile the top card, you may play it this turn / until the end of your next turn"; `exileTop`, `o.playUntil`; those cards show at the end of your hand with a red edge and can be cast or played as a land), **storm** (copies for each spell cast before it this turn, `G.castThisTurn`), **cascade** (`cascade`), **delve** and **affinity for artifacts** and "costs {N} less to cast for each ..." (all in `costOf`), **extra turns** (`G.extra`), Equip costs with colored mana (`R.equip` is a cost now), +1/+1 counters on "target artifact or creature you control", "triggers only once each turn", "Activate only during your turn", ability words stripped ("Metalcraft —"), "As ~ enters, choose a creature type" with lords "of the chosen type" (the most common type in your deck is chosen; `commonType`), "look at the top card of your library any time" (shown as 👁 in your bar). Abilities now pay with other mana first, so a creature land doesn't tap itself for its own cost.
  - **Coverage** (2026-10-06): fully automated 19.3% of all paper cards and 29.1% of the top 1,000 (from 28.5%); castable 83.4% of the top 1,000.

- **Rules round 5 (2026-10-06):** **protection** from colors, creatures, artifacts or everything (`pro:W` etc. from `splitKw`; `protFrom` blocks targeting, blocking and damage; Mother of Runes picks the opponent's main color), **mana abilities with costs** ("{1}, {T}: Add {R}{G}", "Sacrifice a creature: Add {C}{C}", "Add {G} for each Elf you control"; they add to the mana pool at once, no stack, 605.3), **"up to N targets"** (the first target at cast, the rest one at a time when it resolves; you can stop), **optional costs** ("you may pay {2} / pay N life / sacrifice a creature / discard a card. If you do, ... If you don't, ..."; `optionalCost`, `ifDo` / `ifDont`), **Equipment and Aura triggers** ("whenever equipped creature deals combat damage to a player / attacks / dies"; the Swords), **"when ~ is put into a graveyard from the battlefield"** (Rancor), **hand disruption** (Duress, Thoughtseize, Inquisition: you see their hand and pick; Hymn and Mind Twist at random), "don't untap during the next untap step" (Frost Breath), "untap all lands you control".
  - **Coverage** (2026-10-06): fully automated 20.7% of all paper cards and 32.8% of the top 1,000 (from 29.1%); castable 83.5%. The rest is mostly conditional text ("as long as", "if you control"), protection, equipment combat triggers, "When ~ is put into a graveyard", transform / double-faced cards and multi-target effects.

- **Rules round 6 (2026-10-06):**
  - **Two-faced and two-part cards:** Scryfall's back face is kept (`slimFace`, `card.back`, id `<id>-b`, card cache `v: 3`; older cached cards are fetched again). Transform cards flip with "transform ~" and "exile ~, then return it transformed" (711.8: back to the front face in every other zone). Modal double-faced cards, adventures and split cards offer the other half in the card popup (`humanCastBack`). An adventure goes to exile and the creature can be cast from there (715.4; `o.advReady`). An MDFC land back can be played as your land. The AI weighs both halves (`aiBestPlay`).
  - **Conditions** (`parseCond` / `condOk`): a condition is read once into a test and checked when it matters.
    - Where they apply: "If COND, effect" (`e.cond`), "If COND, effect instead" (`e.alt`, `e.condN` for damage), "deals N damage instead if COND", intervening-if triggers (603.4, `tr.cond`), "As long as COND, ~ gets +N/+N / has ... / isn't a creature" (`R.selfCond`, in `buffsOn` and `isCreature`), conditional anthems, and "If you control a commander, you may cast ~ without paying its mana cost" (`R.freeIf`, in `costOf`).
    - Conditions read: you control a commander / N or more X / an X / no X; your life or an opponent's; cards in hand or graveyard; delirium card types; devotion; counters on ~; it's (not) your turn; a creature died / you gained life / you attacked / you cast N spells this turn; spells cast last turn; an opponent controls more creatures or lands; ~ is tapped.
    - Turn tracking: `G.castBy`, `G.lastCastBy`, `G.diedTurn`, `P.gainedTurn`, `G.attackedTurn`.
    - Werewolves transform at upkeep ("no spells were cast last turn"). Daybound / nightbound are played the same way, per card (marked Partly). Delver of Secrets reveals and transforms (`revealTransform`).
  - **Modes:** "Choose two —", "one or both", "one or more", "up to two" and Akroma's / Jeska's Will with a commander pick several modes (`R.modeN`, `modeRange`, `pickModes`, `aiPickModes`; `o.mode` can be a list).
  - **Planeswalkers can be attacked:** if the opponent has one, you pick what each attacker attacks (`chooseAttackTargets`; 508.1b). The AI sends spare attackers at planeswalkers it can finish (`aiAimAtPlaneswalkers`). Damage to a planeswalker removes loyalty (120.3c). "Any target" includes planeswalkers.
  - **Divided damage** (Arc Lightning, Forked Bolt, Fire): each target gets at least 1 (601.2d). You add targets one at a time and type the split; the AI kills what it can and sends the rest at you (`divideTargets`, `splitDamage`).
  - **Overload** (702.96): the spell is read again with "target" as "each" (`R.overload`). The popup offers "Cast with overload", and the AI weighs it. Cyclonic Rift, Vandalblast, Mizzium Mortars, Damn.
  - **Smaller additions:**
    - Lands: shock lands ("pay 2 life or it enters tapped"; you're asked, the AI pays when it has a play) and reveal lands (untapped when you hold the land type). "Play an additional land" (static and one turn, `landLimit`). Karoo lands return a land. "Untap up to N lands".
    - Triggers: "whenever an opponent / a player casts" (with color filters; "their first ... spell each turn"), "whenever a creature you control attacks" (once per attacker), "whenever one or more other creatures die".
    - Effects: "unless that player pays {N}" (the opponent chooses), "exile that creature" / "put a counter on it" after a targeted effect (`target: 'last'`), "tap all creatures your opponents control", mass bounce, "each creature deals N damage to its controller", "Damage can't be prevented this turn".
    - Filters: Pyroblast-style "if it's blue" (played as targeting only a blue spell or permanent), "target X you don't control", protection from all colors.
    - "You have no maximum hand size".
  - **Coverage** (2026-10-06): fully automated 22.7% of all paper cards and 39.4% of the top 1,000 (from 32.8%); castable 84.8% of the top 1,000. Largest remaining gaps: Class levels, "look at the top N" variants, Spree, Station, filter lands, "costs {N} less" for spell types, copy effects that choose new targets, and "*/*" creatures that count other things.
  - **Coverage** (measured on 2026-10-05 over all 33,225 paper cards from Scryfall, by running `rulesFor` on each in the browser; "top" = most-played on EDHREC): fully automated 8% -> 18% of all cards and 13% -> 25% of the top 1,000; castable (fully or partly) 76% -> 82.5% of the top 1,000. The biggest remaining groups: conditional text ("if", "as long as"), "look at the top N cards", additional costs (kicker, convoke, delve, evoke), mana abilities with extra costs, power that depends on something (*/*), Sagas, and multi-target effects. Rerun the measurement after each round.
