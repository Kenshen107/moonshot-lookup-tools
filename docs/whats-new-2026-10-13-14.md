# What's new in Spellslinger Duels (Oct 13-14, 2026)

Spellslinger Duels is a fan-made test game: open packs, build decks and play Magic against computer opponents. It is a test build, free to play, with no real money. Here is what got better over the last two days.

## 4-player Commander
- Play a real free-for-all pod: you plus 3 computer players, 40 life, 21 commander damage, last player standing wins. Pick 1 vs 1, 3 players or 4 players on the Play tab.
- A strip on the table shows each opponent's life, commander damage, hand, creatures and whose turn it is. It works on phones too.
- Pods use the multiplayer mulligan: your first mulligan is free.
- You see a pop-up when any player casts or attacks, just like in a duel.

## Computer opponents with personalities
- Seven playstyles: Aggro, Control, Midrange, Ramp, Tokens, Voltron and Spellslinger. Each one builds its deck, attacks, blocks and picks targets differently.
- "Surprise me" builds a brand-new random Commander or Modern deck from cards the game fully runs, so every game is different.

## Politics, like a real table
- **Archenemy:** whoever gets far ahead becomes the table's target. Everyone piles on them, and a 🎯 marks them.
- **Feelings:** bots get worried, angry, confident or smug, shown by an emoji, and speech bubbles pop up on screen saying why ("You went after my stuff").
- **Deals:** make a Peace deal, a Hands off deal, or Gang up on a third player. Bots answer based on trust, grudges and their playstyle, keep their word, and remember when you break it. Break a deal twice and nobody will deal with you again. Bots can also offer you peace when they're in trouble.
- Table Drain decks (the "each opponent loses life" kind) are now seen as a threat sooner, so one deck type doesn't win everything.

## Rules fixes you asked for
- **Commander death:** when your commander dies or is exiled, you choose whether it goes back to the command zone, so reanimating it works.
- **Discarding:** discarding to hand size, looting and discard costs now let you choose the card.
- **Saddle and Crew:** you pick which creatures to tap, and you can saddle a mount more than once in a turn.
- **Choosing cards:** abilities that return a card from a graveyard now list only cards that fit, and can't be used with none.

## More cards work
- Three rounds of work added about 750 cards that the game now runs fully, including Morph, Suspend, Ninjutsu, Plot, Disturb, Retrace, Embalm, Squad, Casualty, Enlist, Renown, Modular, Mentor, Training and many more.
- A "frequency sweep" now lists which unread card wordings block the most cards, so the biggest gaps get fixed first.

## Testing (for the curious)
- A new offline test lab plays hundreds of computer-vs-computer games a minute with no internet, and any game can be replayed from its seed.
- It found and fixed several real bugs: games that froze, a duplicated card, and abilities with no cost. Thousands of games now run with no hangs, errors or broken rules.
- 800 four-player pods with six political deck styles showed betrayals and shifting archenemies make the most entertaining games. Write-up: `docs/spellslinger-political-decks.md`.

## Other tools (Oct 13)
- **Buylist:** optional per-card cash and credit rates for premium cards.
- **Pokémon tool:** a new Products tab explaining each kind of sealed product, with store search links.
