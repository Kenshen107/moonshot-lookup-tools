Welcome to Spellslinger Duels

A test game: open packs with free coins, build a deck, and play Magic against an opponent playing a real deck theme.

How it works

  1. Packs: spend coins on packs from any set, Alpha to today. What's inside follows the real pack odds (from MTGJSON).
  2. Decks: build a Modern deck (60 cards, up to 4 of each) or a Commander deck (100 cards, one of each, plus a commander). Basic lands are free. Or grab a ready-made starter deck.
  3. Play: pick an opponent. Modern opponents play themed decks built from Scryfall; Commander opponents play EDHREC's average deck for this week's trending commanders.
  4. Earn: 150 coins for a win (200 in Commander), 40 for trying.
  5. Shop: run a card shop. Put cards in your display case, haggle with customers, and close up each day (rent is due). Each card you open has a condition, and a rare few are serialized.

Your stats

What the rules engine does (and doesn't, yet)

This is a base model. It plays real turns: untap, draw, a land a turn, mana from lands and mana rocks, summoning sickness, combat with attackers and blockers, and Commander damage and commander tax. These keywords work: flying, reach, trample, first strike, double strike, deathtouch, lifelink, vigilance, haste, defender, menace, indestructible and hexproof. Card text is read automatically: damage, destroy / exile / bounce, draw, life gain and loss, pump spells, +1/+1 counters, tapping, tokens, discard, auras and equipment that give +N/+N, and "when this enters" effects.

Every card shows how much of its text the game can run: Automated Partly Not yet. "Partly" cards still play, but the text the game can't read does nothing. "Not yet" cards (planeswalkers, X spells and most complex text) can't be cast yet. Spells and "when this enters" abilities use the stack: the other player can respond with instants (including counterspells) before each one resolves, and a spell whose target is gone doesn't resolve. The 📖 Rules tab has the official Comprehensive Rules to look things up.
